Yes — this is a real, legitimate service, often called a daily money manager, and it's more accessible than people assume.

## What These Services Actually Do
A daily money manager handles bill payments, account reconciliation, and basic budgeting oversight — distinct from a financial advisor, who focuses on investment and long-term planning rather than day-to-day transactions.

## Typical Cost
Rates vary by scope, but hourly or flat monthly fees are common, generally more affordable than a full financial advisor relationship since the service is narrower.

## Who Actually Benefits
This service fits people managing a parent's finances, those overwhelmed by multiple accounts, or anyone whose bill-paying errors have caused real financial damage — not necessarily high earners.

## The Bottom Line
If missed or late bill payments are a recurring problem, a dedicated money manager addresses that specific gap more directly than a general financial advisor would.
