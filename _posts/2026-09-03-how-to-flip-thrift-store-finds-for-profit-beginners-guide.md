Thrift flipping rewards pattern recognition more than luck — knowing what sells and where it sells highest is the actual skill.

## Know Your Resale Platforms Before You Shop
Different platforms favor different categories — vintage clothing often performs best on dedicated resale apps, while household items and electronics may do better on general marketplaces.

## Learn to Spot Brand and Quality Signals
Recognizing valuable brands, materials, and construction quality at a glance is what separates profitable flippers from people who buy things that don't resell.

## Price Research Before You Buy, Not After
Quickly checking completed/sold listings for similar items before purchasing prevents buying things that look valuable but have weak actual resale demand.

## Photography Sells Items More Than Descriptions Do
Clean, well-lit photos on a simple background consistently outperform cluttered or dark photos, regardless of how good the written description is.

## The Bottom Line
Profitable thrift flipping comes from research and pattern recognition — knowing what sells and where, before you ever make a purchase.
