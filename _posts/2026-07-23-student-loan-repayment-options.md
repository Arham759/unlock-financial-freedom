Student loan repayment options have expanded significantly, making the "right" approach dependent on individual income and career situation.

## Standard vs. Extended Repayment
Standard plans pay off loans faster with higher payments; extended plans lower monthly payments but increase total interest paid over time.

## Income-Driven Repayment Plans
These plans tie monthly payments to income, which can provide significant short-term relief, though they generally extend the total repayment timeline.

## Refinancing Considerations
Refinancing can lower interest rates for well-qualified borrowers, but often means giving up federal protections and income-driven options — worth weighing carefully.

## Forgiveness Program Eligibility
Certain careers and repayment plans may qualify for loan forgiveness after a set period — eligibility rules are specific and worth verifying directly rather than assuming.

## The Bottom Line
The right repayment strategy depends heavily on current income stability and career path — this is general information, not a substitute for reviewing your specific loan terms.
