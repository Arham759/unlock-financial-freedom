description: "The five foundational personal finance concepts that underpin every other money decision."

Most personal finance advice builds on five core ideas — understanding these makes everything else easier to evaluate.

## 1. Spend Less Than You Earn
The single most important rule, and the one most other strategies exist to support.

## 2. Build an Emergency Fund First
A cash buffer prevents a single unexpected expense from becoming debt.

## 3. Pay Off High-Interest Debt Aggressively
High-interest debt grows faster than most investments, making payoff a near-guaranteed return.

## 4. Invest for the Long Term
Starting early and staying consistent matters more than picking perfect investments.

## 5. Protect What You've Built
Insurance and estate basics prevent a single event from undoing years of progress.

## The Bottom Line
These five ideas, in order, form the skeleton that every other financial strategy hangs on.
