Broad blogs compete with massive established sites. Micro-niche blogs compete with almost no one — if you pick the right angle.

## What Makes a Niche "Micro" Enough
Instead of "personal finance," something like "budgeting for travel nurses" or "money management for new parents of twins" narrows your competition dramatically while still having real search volume.

## Why Narrow Beats Broad for New Sites
Google weighs topical authority — a small site that thoroughly covers one specific area can outrank a much bigger, more generic site on that topic.

## Content Depth Matters More Than Post Count
Ten genuinely thorough posts that fully answer a specific question outperform fifty thin posts that skim the surface of many topics.

## Internal Linking Builds Authority Faster Than You'd Expect
Linking your own related posts together helps Google understand your site's topical focus and helps readers go deeper — both improve ranking potential over time.

## The Bottom Line
A tightly focused micro-niche with genuinely thorough content is a realistic path to ranking, even for a brand-new site with no backlinks yet.
