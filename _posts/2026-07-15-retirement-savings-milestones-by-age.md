Age-based savings milestones offer a useful rough check, though they shouldn't be treated as strict pass/fail targets.

## Why These Benchmarks Exist
Common guidelines suggest savings targets as a multiple of annual salary at different ages, designed to stay roughly on track for a traditional retirement timeline.

## Why Individual Circumstances Vary Significantly
Career start timing, income trajectory, and regional cost of living all meaningfully shift what's realistic for any individual compared to a generic benchmark.

## Using Milestones as Direction, Not Judgment
These benchmarks are most useful as a general direction check — "am I roughly on track" — rather than a strict score to feel anxious about.

## The Bottom Line
Age-based savings milestones are a reasonable rough guide, but your own trajectory and adjustments matter more than hitting a generic number exactly.
