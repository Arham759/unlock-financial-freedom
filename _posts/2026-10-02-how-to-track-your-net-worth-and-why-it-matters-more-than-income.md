---

Income gets all the attention, but net worth is the number that actually reflects financial progress over time.

## Income vs. Net Worth
A high income with high debt and no savings can mean a lower net worth than a modest income with consistent saving and low debt — income alone doesn't tell the full story.

## What Net Worth Actually Measures
Net worth is simply assets (what you own) minus liabilities (what you owe) — a single number that captures the full financial picture rather than one slice of it.

## Tracking It Doesn't Need to Be Complicated
A simple spreadsheet updated monthly or quarterly, listing account balances and debts, is enough to see meaningful trends over time without needing specialized software.

## Why the Trend Matters More Than the Number
A single net worth snapshot means little on its own — what matters is whether the number is trending up over months and years, regardless of the starting point.

## The Bottom Line
Tracking net worth over time gives a far more honest picture of financial progress than focusing on income alone.
