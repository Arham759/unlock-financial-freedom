Facebook monetization sounds simple on paper and gets confusing fast once you're actually trying to qualify. Here's what matters.

## In-Stream Ads
Video content longer than 3 minutes can carry mid-roll ads once your Page meets watch-time and follower thresholds. This is the most direct path but requires consistent long-form video output.

## Stars and Fan Subscriptions
Live content and loyal-audience features let followers directly tip or subscribe. This works best for creators with an engaged, returning audience rather than viral one-off content.

## Facebook's Content Monetization Program Has Replaced Several Older Tools
Facebook has consolidated multiple monetization paths into unified eligibility requirements — always check your Page's Professional Dashboard for your specific current eligibility rather than relying on older guides.

## What Actually Gets Pages Demonetized
Reused content, misleading thumbnails, and engagement bait are the most common reasons pages lose monetization status — originality matters more than Facebook lets on.

## The Bottom Line
Facebook monetization rewards consistency and originality over virality — a smaller, loyal audience watching full videos outperforms a large, disengaged one.
