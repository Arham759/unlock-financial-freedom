---
title: "Should I Hire a Financial Advisor or Use a Budget App?"
description: "Comparing the use cases for a human financial advisor versus a budgeting app."
---

These aren't always competing choices — understanding what each actually does clarifies when one, both, or neither fits your situation.

## What Budget Apps Do Well
Apps excel at tracking, categorizing, and visualizing day-to-day spending — tasks that are largely mechanical and don't require personalized judgment.

## What They Can't Replace
Complex decisions — tax strategy, retirement account selection, major life transitions — benefit from personalized human judgment an app can't provide.

## A Reasonable Combination
Many people use an app for daily tracking while consulting an advisor periodically for bigger-picture decisions, rather than choosing one exclusively.

## The Bottom Line
Simple, mechanical tracking suits an app; complex, personal decisions suit a human advisor — the right choice often includes both.
