title: "Credit Score 101: What Actually Moves the Number"
description: "A clear explanation of the factors that actually influence your credit score and their relative weight."
---

Credit scores feel like a black box, but the actual factors behind the number are well documented — most people just focus on the wrong ones.

## Payment History Is the Biggest Factor
Consistently paying at least the minimum on time matters more than almost anything else in the scoring formula — a single missed payment can do real damage.

## Credit Utilization Matters More Than People Think
Using a high percentage of your available credit limit — even if you pay it off monthly — can lower your score. Keeping utilization well under 30% generally helps.

## Length of Credit History Can't Be Rushed
Older accounts help your score simply by existing longer. This is why closing your oldest credit card, even an unused one, can sometimes hurt more than help.

## New Credit Inquiries Have a Small, Temporary Impact
Applying for new credit causes a small dip that recovers over time — it's rarely the dominant factor people assume it is.

## The Bottom Line
Two habits drive most of your score: paying on time and keeping utilization low — everything else is secondary.
