title: "Side Income Tax Deductions"
description: "Legitimate tax deductions available for people earning side income, beyond general tax advice."
---

Side income comes with its own set of potential deductions that many beginners miss simply from not knowing to track them.

## Home Office Deduction
A portion of home expenses may be deductible if a specific space is used regularly and exclusively for the side business.

## Equipment and Software
Tools, software subscriptions, and equipment used specifically for the side income activity are often legitimately deductible business expenses.

## Mileage and Travel
Business-related travel and mileage, properly tracked, can represent a meaningful deduction often missed without consistent record-keeping throughout the year.

## Why Tracking Throughout the Year Matters
Attempting to reconstruct expenses at tax time, rather than tracking consistently, usually means missing legitimate deductions simply from incomplete records.

## The Bottom Line
Consistent expense tracking throughout the year, not just at tax time, is what actually captures the deductions side income earners are entitled to — this is general information, not personalized tax advice.
