---
title: "How Often Should You Meet With Your Financial Advisor?"
description: "Realistic expectations for how frequently clients should meet with their financial advisor."
---

Meeting frequency expectations vary, but a few general patterns apply across most advisor relationships.

## Annual Reviews Are the Baseline
At minimum, an annual comprehensive review ensures your plan still matches your current goals and life situation.

## Major Life Events Warrant Extra Check-Ins
Marriage, a new child, a job change, or an inheritance are all reasonable triggers for an additional meeting outside the regular schedule.

## More Frequent Isn't Always Better
Excessively frequent meetings don't necessarily improve outcomes and can sometimes indicate an advisor overly focused on short-term activity rather than long-term strategy.

## The Bottom Line
Annual reviews plus check-ins around major life changes cover most people's actual needs — more frequent contact isn't automatically more valuable.
