The personal finance field includes a wider range of career paths than just "financial advisor."

## Financial Planning and Advising
The most visible path, involving direct client relationships around investment, retirement, and life planning.

## Financial Coaching and Education
A growing field focused more on behavioral change and financial literacy than investment management specifically.

## Credit Counseling
Helping people navigate debt and credit repair, often through nonprofit or community-based organizations.

## Content and Media
Financial writers, educators, and creators build careers explaining personal finance concepts to broad audiences.

## The Bottom Line
Personal finance careers range from highly technical advisory roles to education-focused paths — the right fit depends on whether you're drawn to numbers or communication.
