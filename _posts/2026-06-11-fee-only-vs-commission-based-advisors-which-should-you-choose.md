This distinction affects more than cost — it shapes what an advisor is actually incentivized to recommend.

## How Fee-Only Advisors Are Paid
Fee-only advisors charge directly for their advice — hourly, flat fee, or percentage of assets — with no commissions from product sales.

## How Commission-Based Advisors Are Paid
These advisors earn money when you buy specific financial products they recommend, which can create a conflict between what's good for you and what pays them more.

## Why This Distinction Matters So Much
A commission structure doesn't guarantee bad advice, but it does create an incentive worth being aware of when evaluating recommendations.

## The Bottom Line
Fee-only advisors generally align incentives more closely with client outcomes — worth prioritizing when the option is available.
