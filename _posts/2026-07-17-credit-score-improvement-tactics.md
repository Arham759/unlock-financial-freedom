title: "Credit Score Improvement Tactics"
description: "Specific, actionable tactics for improving a credit score beyond general advice."
---

Beyond the basics of paying on time, a few specific tactics can meaningfully accelerate credit score improvement.

## Request a Credit Limit Increase
Increasing your credit limit without increasing spending lowers your utilization ratio, which can improve your score relatively quickly.

## Dispute Errors on Your Credit Report
Credit reports contain errors more often than people assume — disputing and correcting inaccuracies can produce a meaningful score improvement.

## Keep Old Accounts Open
Closing old, unused credit accounts can shorten your average credit history length and raise utilization — generally better to keep them open and inactive.

## Diversify Credit Types Carefully
A mix of credit types (revolving and installment) can modestly help your score, though this shouldn't drive taking on debt you don't otherwise need.

## The Bottom Line
Beyond consistent on-time payment, utilization management and report accuracy are the fastest levers for meaningful credit score improvement.
