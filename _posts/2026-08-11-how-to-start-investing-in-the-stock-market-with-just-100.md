---
title: "How to Start Investing in the Stock Market With Just $100"
description: "A beginner-friendly guide to starting stock market investing with a small amount of money."
---

You don't need thousands of dollars to start investing — you need a plan that matches the amount you actually have.

## Fractional Shares Changed the Math
Most major brokerages now let you buy fractional shares, meaning $100 can buy a slice of expensive stocks like major tech companies instead of being locked out entirely.

## Index Funds Are the Low-Effort Starting Point
A broad market index fund spreads your $100 across hundreds of companies instantly, reducing the risk of picking one bad individual stock while you're still learning.

## Automate Small, Regular Contributions
Consistently investing a small amount monthly (even $25–$50) tends to outperform trying to time a single large investment, because it smooths out market ups and downs over time.

## Understand What You're Actually Buying
Before investing even a small amount, know whether you're buying a single company, a fund, or a sector — this single habit prevents most beginner mistakes.

## The Bottom Line
Starting small isn't a disadvantage — it's a low-risk way to build the habit and knowledge that matters more than your starting balance.
