---
title: "How to Start Freelance Writing With Zero Experience"
description: "A practical starting guide for people who want to freelance write but have no portfolio or clients yet."
---

The "you need experience to get experience" problem is real in freelance writing, but it's solvable with the right first moves.

## Build a Portfolio Before You Have Clients
Write 3-5 sample pieces specifically for the niche you want to work in. Unpaid spec pieces built deliberately for your target niche are more useful early on than a scattered blog.

## Pick a Narrow Niche, Not "General Writing"
"I write about anything" is a weak pitch. "I write SaaS onboarding emails" or "I write real estate listing descriptions" gives a potential client a specific reason to hire you over someone else.

## Where Beginner Writers Actually Land First Clients
Content agencies, smaller blogs, and niche newsletters tend to hire less-experienced writers at lower rates than major publications — a realistic and useful starting point.

## Price Based on Value, Not Just Time
Charging per word undervalues strategic or well-researched work. As you gain experience, shifting to per-project or value-based pricing tends to pay better for the same effort.

## The Bottom Line
A focused niche and a few deliberately built samples beat a vague pitch and an empty portfolio every time.
