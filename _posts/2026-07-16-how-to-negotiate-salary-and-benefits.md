---
title: "How to Negotiate Salary and Benefits"
description: "A practical approach to negotiating both salary and non-salary benefits during a job offer."
---

Salary negotiation gets most of the attention, but benefits negotiation is often equally valuable and less commonly attempted.

## Research Before You Negotiate Anything
Knowing a realistic market range for the specific role and location grounds your negotiation in data rather than guesswork.

## Negotiate the Full Package, Not Just Base Salary
Signing bonuses, additional vacation time, remote flexibility, and professional development budgets are all negotiable and sometimes easier to secure than a higher base.

## Let Them Name a Number First When Possible
Stating your value and letting the employer propose a number first can sometimes lead to a higher starting point than anchoring first yourself.

## Get the Final Offer in Writing
Verbal agreements on negotiated terms should always be confirmed in writing before accepting, to avoid later misunderstandings.

## The Bottom Line
Negotiating the full compensation package, not just base salary, consistently captures more value than most candidates realize is available.
