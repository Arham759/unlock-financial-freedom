---
title: "How Much Does It Cost to Hire a Financial Manager?"
description: "A breakdown of typical financial advisor fee structures and what drives the cost up or down."
---

Advisor costs vary more than most beginners expect, largely based on how the advisor is compensated.

## Fee-Only vs. Percentage of Assets
Some advisors charge a flat fee regardless of your portfolio size; others charge a percentage of assets under management, which scales with your balance.

## Commission-Based Costs Are Often Hidden
Commission-based advisors may appear "free" upfront but earn money through product commissions, which can create a conflict of interest in what they recommend.

## What Drives Price Up
More complex situations — business ownership, multiple income sources, estate planning needs — typically cost more than a straightforward single-income household.

## The Bottom Line
Understanding how an advisor is paid matters as much as the number itself — it reveals potential incentives behind their recommendations.
