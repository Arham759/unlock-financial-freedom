The "best" debt strategy on paper isn't always the one that actually gets debt paid off in practice.

## Interest-Rate-Based Strategies
Prioritizing the highest-interest debt first saves the most money mathematically, but requires sustained discipline over what can be a slow initial process.

## Balance-Based Strategies
Prioritizing the smallest balance first builds early momentum through visible wins, often improving follow-through even at a slightly higher total interest cost.

## Consolidation as a Structural Tool
Combining multiple debts into a single lower-rate payment can simplify tracking and sometimes reduce total interest, though it requires discipline not to accumulate new debt afterward.

## The Bottom Line
The most effective strategy is the one that keeps you consistently paying extra month after month — behavioral fit matters as much as the math.
