---
title: "What Does a Personal Financial Advisor Actually Do?"
description: "A clear breakdown of what a financial advisor's job actually involves, beyond picking stocks."
---

"Financial advisor" covers more ground than most people expect, and understanding the actual scope helps you know what to ask for.

## Beyond Investment Picks
A good advisor helps with retirement planning, tax-efficient account strategy, insurance gaps, and major life decisions like buying a home — not just portfolio selection.

## The Ongoing Relationship
Advisors typically meet periodically to reassess goals as income, family situation, or market conditions change, rather than setting a plan once and disappearing.

## What They Don't Do
Advisors generally don't manage day-to-day bill paying or budgeting minutiae — that's a different service entirely (see daily money managers).

## The Bottom Line
A financial advisor's real value is ongoing strategic planning across your whole financial picture, not just stock selection.
