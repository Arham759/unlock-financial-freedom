---
title: "How Much Should I Budget for Each Category of Spending"
description: "Practical guidance on allocating a budget across major spending categories."
---

Generic percentage guidelines offer a useful starting point, but your actual numbers should reflect your real cost of living.

## Housing Typically Takes the Largest Share
A commonly cited guideline suggests keeping housing costs around 30% of income, though this varies significantly by region.

## Transportation Often Gets Underestimated
Beyond a car payment, insurance, fuel, and maintenance add up to a meaningful recurring category many budgets underestimate.

## Discretionary Spending Needs a Real Number, Not Zero
Budgets that allocate nothing to discretionary spending tend to fail — a modest, honest allowance improves long-term adherence.

## The Bottom Line
Use percentage guidelines as a starting reference point, then adjust based on your actual regional costs and priorities.
