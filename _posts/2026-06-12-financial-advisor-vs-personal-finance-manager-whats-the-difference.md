---
title: "Financial Advisor vs. Personal Finance Manager: What's the Difference"
description: "Clarifying the distinct roles of financial advisors and personal finance managers."
---

These titles get used loosely, but they typically describe genuinely different services.

## Financial Advisors Focus on Strategy
Advisors typically handle investment strategy, retirement planning, and big-picture financial decisions over time.

## Personal Finance Managers Handle Execution
This role (sometimes called a daily money manager) handles day-to-day tasks — bill payment, account reconciliation, budget tracking.

## Some Overlap Exists
Certain firms or professionals blend both roles, so it's worth directly asking what's included rather than assuming based on title alone.

## The Bottom Line
Match the service to your actual need — strategic planning calls for an advisor, day-to-day execution calls for a money manager.
