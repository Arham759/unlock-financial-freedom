---
title: "Financial Wellness in Your 20s, 30s, 40s, 50s, and 60s"
description: "How financial priorities shift across each decade of adult life."
---

Financial priorities genuinely shift decade to decade — treating every stage the same way misses what actually matters most at each point.

## Your 20s: Build Habits
Starting retirement contributions, building credit, and establishing an emergency fund matter more in this decade than optimizing for maximum returns.

## Your 30s: Balance Growth and Stability
Career growth often accelerates alongside family formation, making balancing increased earning with increased obligations the central challenge.

## Your 40s: Catch Up and Protect
Peak earning years often coincide with the most room to catch up on savings, alongside increased need for insurance and estate planning.

## Your 50s and Beyond: Shift Toward Preservation
Priorities generally shift from aggressive growth toward protecting accumulated wealth and finalizing a realistic retirement timeline.

## The Bottom Line
Financial priorities should evolve deliberately by decade — the right strategy at 25 isn't the right strategy at 55, and vice versa.
