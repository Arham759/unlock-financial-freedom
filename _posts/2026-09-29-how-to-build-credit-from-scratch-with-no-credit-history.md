---
title: "How to Build Credit From Scratch With No Credit History"
description: "A practical guide for people starting completely new to building credit with no prior history."
---

Having no credit history creates a frustrating catch-22 — lenders want history you can't get without already having credit.

## Secured Credit Cards Are the Most Common Starting Point
These require a cash deposit that typically becomes your credit limit, letting you build payment history with minimal risk to the lender while you establish a track record.

## Becoming an Authorized User
Being added as an authorized user on a trusted family member's long-standing, well-managed credit card can transfer some of that positive history to your own report.

## Credit-Builder Loans
Some financial institutions offer small loans specifically designed to build credit, where the loan amount is held until it's paid off, reporting payment history along the way.

## Consistency Matters More Than Credit Limit Size
A small credit limit used responsibly and paid on time builds credit just as effectively as a larger one — the limit size matters far less than payment consistency.

## The Bottom Line
Building credit from zero is a slow, steady process — the method matters less than consistent, on-time payment history over several months.
