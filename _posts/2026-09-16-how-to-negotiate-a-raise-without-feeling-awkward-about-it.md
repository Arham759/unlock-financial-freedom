---
title: "How to Negotiate a Raise Without Feeling Awkward About It"
description: "A practical approach to asking for a raise with confidence, even if the conversation feels uncomfortable."
---

The discomfort around asking for a raise is almost universal — preparation is what actually makes the conversation easier, not confidence alone.

## Build Your Case With Specifics, Not Feelings
"I've been here a while" is weak. A list of specific completed projects, measurable results, or added responsibilities since your last review is a far stronger foundation.

## Time It Around Value, Not Just the Calendar
Raising the topic shortly after a clear win or deliverable lands better than raising it during a routine, unrelated check-in.

## Practice the Actual Ask Out Loud
Rehearsing the specific sentence that opens the conversation reduces in-the-moment anxiety more than just thinking through the general topic.

## Have a Number, But Let Them Respond First
Knowing your target number matters, but opening with a clear statement of your contributions before stating a number often leads to a more productive conversation.

## The Bottom Line
Specific evidence and preparation reduce the emotional weight of the conversation far more than trying to feel more confident first.
