Print-on-demand removes the biggest traditional barrier to starting a product business — inventory risk — but it comes with its own tradeoffs.

## How Print-on-Demand Actually Works
A supplier prints and ships your designs only after a customer orders, meaning you never hold physical stock or pay for products upfront.

## Why Generic Designs Struggle
Broad, generic designs compete directly with thousands of similar listings. Designs built for a specific, identifiable audience (a profession, a hobby, a regional identity) convert noticeably better.

## Margins Are Thinner Than They Look
Because the supplier handles printing and shipping, per-item profit margins are lower than traditional retail — success depends on sales volume more than markup percentage.

## Platform Choice Affects Discoverability
Some platforms function more like marketplaces with built-in search traffic, while others require you to drive all your own traffic — understanding this difference shapes your whole strategy.

## The Bottom Line
Print-on-demand lowers the barrier to starting a product business, but success still depends on niche-specific design and realistic margin expectations.
