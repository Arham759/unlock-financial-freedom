You don't need a design degree to make money with Canva — you need to package what you already know how to do.

## Sell Canva Templates
Social media templates, resume templates, and planner templates sell consistently on Etsy and Gumroad. Buyers want something they can customize in minutes, not a blank canvas.

## Offer "Canva-Only" Design Services
Many small business owners want simple flyers, menus, or social posts and don't need a full agency — just someone faster than they are at Canva. Position yourself as the quick, affordable option.

## Repurpose Content for Creators
Creators constantly need their long-form content turned into carousels, quote graphics, and thumbnails. This is Canva-native work that doesn't require advanced design skill, just good taste and consistency.

## Build a Template Shop Around One Niche
Generic templates compete with thousands of others. A shop focused specifically on, say, real estate agents or wedding planners stands out and can charge more.

## The Bottom Line
Canva monetization rewards packaging and niche focus over raw design talent — most successful sellers aren't the most "creative," they're the most specific.
