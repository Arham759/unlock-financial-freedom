title: "Tax Help for Low-Income Filers: Free Services Available"
description: "An overview of free tax preparation resources available for low-income filers."
---

Tax preparation costs can feel like an unfair burden on exactly the people who can least afford them — but legitimate free options exist.

## Volunteer Income Tax Assistance Programs
Community-based volunteer tax prep programs offer free filing help for qualifying lower-income individuals, often staffed by IRS-certified volunteers.

## Free Filing Software Options
Several major tax software providers offer genuinely free filing for simple returns under certain income thresholds — worth checking eligibility directly before paying for software.

## Community and Library Resources
Many local libraries and community centers host tax help events during filing season, often in partnership with established free tax programs.

## The Bottom Line
Free, legitimate tax help exists for qualifying filers — it's worth seeking out rather than assuming paid preparation is the only option.
