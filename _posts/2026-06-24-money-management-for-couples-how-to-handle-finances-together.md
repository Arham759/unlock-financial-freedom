Money is one of the most common sources of relationship conflict — mostly from unclear systems, not necessarily unclear values.

## Full Merge, Full Separate, or Hybrid
Couples broadly choose between fully joint accounts, fully separate finances with shared bill splitting, or a hybrid with both joint and individual accounts — no single approach is universally right.

## Regular Money Conversations Matter More Than the System
Scheduling regular, low-pressure check-ins about finances prevents small disagreements from building into larger conflicts.

## Transparency Doesn't Require Identical Spending Habits
Partners can have different spending styles while still maintaining full transparency about the overall financial picture.

## The Bottom Line
The specific account structure matters less than consistent communication and transparency about the full financial picture.
