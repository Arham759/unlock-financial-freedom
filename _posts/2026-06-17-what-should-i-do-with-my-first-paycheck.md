---
title: "What Should I Do With My First Paycheck?"
description: "Practical priorities for your very first paycheck from a new job."
---

A first paycheck feels like a milestone worth celebrating — a few smart first moves set the tone for everything after it.

## Confirm the Numbers Match Expectations
Checking that your pay, tax withholding, and any benefit deductions match what you expected catches payroll errors early.

## Resist the Urge to Spend It All
A modest celebration is reasonable, but treating the full amount as discretionary spending is a common early misstep.

## Start a Small Automatic Savings Transfer
Setting up even a small automated savings transfer from this very first paycheck builds the habit before spending patterns solidify.

## Check Your Employer Benefits Enrollment Deadlines
Many benefits, including retirement matching, have enrollment windows — missing them can mean leaving real money on the table.

## The Bottom Line
Your first paycheck is less about the amount and more about establishing the habits and checks you'll repeat every pay period after.
