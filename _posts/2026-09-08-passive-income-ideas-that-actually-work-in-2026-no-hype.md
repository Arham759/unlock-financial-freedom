Most "passive income" content oversells the passive part. Here's a more honest breakdown of what actually requires upfront effort versus ongoing effort.

## Dividend Investing: High Upfront Capital, Low Ongoing Effort
Once invested, dividend income requires minimal ongoing work — but it requires meaningful capital to produce meaningful income, making it slow to build from scratch.

## Digital Products: High Upfront Effort, Low Ongoing Effort
Creating a template, course, or guide takes real upfront work, but once built, sales can continue with minimal additional effort beyond occasional marketing.

## Rental Income: High Upfront Capital, Moderate Ongoing Effort
Real estate can generate relatively passive income, but "moderate ongoing effort" (maintenance, tenant issues) is often underestimated in passive income pitches.

## Content Royalties: High Upfront Effort, True Long-Tail Passivity
Content like stock photos, templates, or published writing can continue earning small amounts long after the initial creation work is done.

## The Bottom Line
True passive income almost always requires significant upfront effort or capital — the "passive" part is a payoff for front-loaded work, not an absence of work.
