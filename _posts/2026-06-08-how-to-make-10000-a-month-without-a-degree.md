---
title: "How to Make $10,000 a Month Without a Degree"
description: "A realistic look at income paths that can reach $10,000/month without a college degree."
---

This level of income without a degree is achievable, but it typically requires a specific skill or business, not a single easy path.

## Skilled Trades Scale Further Than Expected
Trades like electrical work or HVAC, especially once running your own crew, can realistically reach this income level with experience.

## Sales Roles With Commission Structures
High-ticket sales positions, especially in tech or real estate, can reach this level faster than most salaried roles, though income is less predictable.

## Service-Based Businesses
Agencies or specialized freelance services (not single-person freelancing, but a small team) can realistically scale to this level with the right niche.

## What to Be Skeptical Of
Any opportunity promising this income with minimal effort or upfront cost is almost certainly not a legitimate path.

## The Bottom Line
Reaching this income without a degree is realistic through trades, sales, or service businesses — but it requires genuine skill-building or business development, not a shortcut.
