---
title: "Insurance Needs Assessment Guide"
description: "A practical framework for assessing which types of insurance coverage you actually need."
---

Insurance needs shift significantly across life stages — a periodic assessment prevents both under- and over-insuring.

## Health Insurance Is the Foundation
Adequate health coverage protects against the single largest potential financial shock for most people, making it the priority starting point.

## Life Insurance Scales With Dependents
The need for life insurance generally scales with how many people financially depend on your income — single individuals with no dependents often need much less than parents.

## Disability Insurance Is Frequently Overlooked
The likelihood of a disabling injury or illness during a working career is often underestimated relative to how much attention it gets in typical financial planning.

## Reassess After Major Life Changes
Marriage, children, a new home, or a significant income change are all reasonable triggers to reassess whether current coverage still fits.

## The Bottom Line
Insurance needs aren't static — a periodic reassessment, especially after major life changes, prevents both costly gaps and unnecessary premium spending.
