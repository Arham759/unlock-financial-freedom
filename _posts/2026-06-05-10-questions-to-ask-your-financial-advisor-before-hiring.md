---
title: "10 Questions to Ask Your Financial Advisor Before Hiring"
description: "Essential vetting questions to ask before committing to a financial advisor."
---

Hiring an advisor without asking the right questions is how people end up with conflicted, expensive advice.

## Are You a Fiduciary?
This single question reveals whether they're legally required to act in your best interest, or just held to a lower "suitability" standard.

## How Are You Compensated?
Fee-only, commission, or a mix changes what incentives shape their recommendations.

## What's Your Investment Philosophy?
Their general approach should match your risk tolerance and goals, not just a generic sales pitch.

## What Happens If I Want to Leave?
Understanding exit terms and any penalties upfront avoids surprises later.

## The Bottom Line
A good advisor welcomes direct questions about fiduciary duty and compensation — hesitation on these is itself a red flag.
