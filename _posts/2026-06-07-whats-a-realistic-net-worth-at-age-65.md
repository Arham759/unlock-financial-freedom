description: "Realistic net worth benchmarks for retirement age and what factors shift the target significantly."

Retirement net worth targets vary enormously based on expected lifestyle and other income sources — there's no single right number.

## Why "25x Expenses" Is a Common Rule of Thumb
A commonly cited guideline suggests having roughly 25 times your expected annual expenses saved, based on a sustainable withdrawal rate — though this varies by individual risk tolerance.

## Social Security and Pensions Change the Math
Guaranteed income sources reduce how much you need from personal savings alone, meaning the "right" net worth depends heavily on these other income streams.

## Housing Status Matters Significantly
A paid-off home dramatically reduces required retirement income compared to ongoing rent or mortgage payments.

## The Bottom Line
Rather than chasing a universal number, calculating your own expected expenses minus other guaranteed income gives a far more accurate personal target.
