TikTok's native monetization tools and brand deals work very differently — and for most creators, one clearly outpays the other.

## How Native Monetization Programs Work
TikTok's built-in creator monetization tools pay based on video performance metrics, but payouts per view are typically small compared to other platforms' ad revenue sharing.

## Why Brand Deals Usually Pay More
A single sponsored post, even with a modest following, often pays more than weeks of native monetization payouts — brands pay for audience relevance and engagement, not raw view count.

## Building Toward Brand Deal Eligibility
Consistent posting in a clear niche, authentic engagement, and a media kit showing your audience demographics make creators far more attractive to brands than follower count alone.

## A Realistic Income Mix
Most sustainable TikTok creators combine small native monetization payouts with periodic brand deals and affiliate links rather than relying on one single income source.

## The Bottom Line
Brand deals typically outpay native monetization tools significantly — building toward brand-ready credibility is usually worth more than chasing platform payout thresholds.
