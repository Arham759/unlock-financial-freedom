This niche career path gets little attention despite being a stable, specialized corner of the finance industry.

## What 401(k) Consultants Actually Do
These professionals help employers design, manage, and comply with retirement plan offerings — a blend of finance knowledge and regulatory expertise.

## Why It's a Stable Niche
Regulatory complexity around retirement plans creates consistent, ongoing demand for specialized expertise that doesn't fluctuate as much as some other finance roles.

## Entry Paths Into the Field
This path often starts in broader benefits administration or financial services roles before specializing specifically in retirement plan consulting.

## The Bottom Line
401(k) consulting offers a specialized, relatively stable career path for those interested in the intersection of finance and employee benefits.
