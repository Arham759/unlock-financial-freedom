Both of these methods work — the real question is which one keeps you motivated enough to actually finish.

## The Avalanche Method
Pay minimums on all debts, then throw extra money at the one with the highest interest rate first. This saves the most money mathematically over time.

## The Snowball Method
Pay minimums on all debts, then throw extra money at the smallest balance first, regardless of interest rate. This builds early motivation through quick wins.

## Why Snowball Often Wins in Practice
Research on real behavior suggests people are more likely to stick with debt payoff when they see balances disappear entirely, even if it costs slightly more in interest overall.

## When Avalanche Makes More Sense
If your highest-interest debt also happens to be a large balance, and you're confident in your discipline, avalanche can save meaningful money without the emotional boost snowball provides.

## The Bottom Line
The "mathematically best" method isn't always the one you'll actually finish — pick the one that keeps you consistent.
