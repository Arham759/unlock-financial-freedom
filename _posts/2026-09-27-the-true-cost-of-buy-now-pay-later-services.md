Buy Now, Pay Later feels different from a credit card, but the underlying risk of overspending works in a very similar way.

## How These Services Actually Work
Most split a purchase into several fixed installments, often advertised as interest-free if paid on schedule — the catch is usually in what happens when a payment is missed.

## The Multiple-Purchase Problem
Because each individual installment feels small, it's easy to accumulate several simultaneous BNPL plans without a clear total picture of what's actually owed across all of them.

## Late Fees and Credit Impact
Missed payments can trigger fees and, with some providers, affect credit reporting — the "interest-free" framing doesn't mean there's no financial consequence to late payment.

## Why It Can Distort Spending Decisions
Splitting a price into smaller numbers can make a purchase feel more affordable than it actually is relative to your real monthly budget.

## The Bottom Line
BNPL isn't inherently bad, but tracking total committed payments across all active plans is essential to avoid it quietly outpacing your actual budget.
