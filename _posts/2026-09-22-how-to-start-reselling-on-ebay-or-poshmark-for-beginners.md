Both platforms support reselling, but they attract different buyers and reward different approaches.

## Platform Differences That Matter
eBay functions more like a general marketplace with auction and fixed-price options; Poshmark is more social and fashion-focused, with buyers who respond to a curated, personal shop feel.

## Sourcing Inventory Before Listing
Successful resellers typically source consistently from thrift stores, clearance sections, or liquidation lots rather than relying on one-off finds — consistency matters more than occasional lucky purchases.

## Listing Quality Drives Conversion
Clear photos from multiple angles, accurate measurements, and honest condition descriptions reduce returns and disputes significantly compared to vague or overly optimistic listings.

## Understanding Fees Before Pricing
Both platforms take a percentage of each sale — factoring this into your pricing from the start prevents the common mistake of underpricing items relative to actual profit.

## The Bottom Line
Consistent sourcing and accurate, high-quality listings matter more to reselling success than which specific platform you choose.
