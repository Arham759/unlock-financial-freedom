Finance rules with catchy names spread quickly online — worth examining whether the underlying logic actually holds up for your situation.

## What the Rule Typically Proposes
Versions of this rule generally suggest splitting savings or spending into specific percentage allocations, similar in spirit to the more common 50/30/20 rule.

## Where Simplified Rules Fall Short
Any fixed-percentage rule assumes a fairly standard income and cost-of-living situation, which doesn't fit everyone equally well.

## When a Simple Rule Is Still Useful
As a rough starting mental model rather than a strict requirement, simplified rules can still help beginners who are otherwise budgeting with no structure at all.

## The Bottom Line
Treat catchy finance rules as a reasonable starting template to adapt, not a strict formula that must be followed exactly.
