---
title: "How to Know If You're Ready for a Financial Advisor"
description: "Signs that indicate it may be time to bring in professional financial advice."
---

There's no universal asset threshold that determines readiness — a few practical signals matter more than account balance alone.

## Complexity Has Outpaced Your Confidence
Multiple income sources, a business, or significant life changes that feel overwhelming to navigate alone are a reasonable trigger.

## You're Making Decisions Based on Guesswork
If major financial decisions are being made without real research or confidence, professional input can meaningfully improve outcomes.

## You Have Assets Worth Protecting
A meaningful amount of accumulated savings or investments raises the stakes of mistakes enough to justify professional guidance.

## You Simply Don't Have the Time
Even straightforward situations can benefit from professional help if the time required for proper DIY management isn't realistically available.

## The Bottom Line
Readiness for an advisor is about complexity and confidence, not a specific asset threshold — it's reasonable to seek help well before feeling "wealthy."
