Affiliate marketing is simpler than the hype around it suggests, but the income timeline is slower than most guides admit.

## How Commissions Actually Work
You get paid a percentage (or flat fee) when someone makes a purchase through your unique tracked link — payment only happens on completed, tracked actions, not clicks or views.

## Why Trust Matters More Than Traffic
A small, engaged audience that trusts your recommendations converts far better than a large audience that doesn't know you. This is why niche focus outperforms broad reach in affiliate income.

## Disclosure Isn't Optional
Clearly disclosing affiliate relationships is both a legal requirement in most places and, practically, builds more trust with your audience than hiding it.

## Realistic Income Timeline
Meaningful affiliate income typically takes months of consistent content before it compounds — this is not a fast income source despite how it's often marketed.

## The Bottom Line
Affiliate marketing pays based on trust and relevance, not volume — a smaller, honest, niche audience usually outperforms a bigger disengaged one.
