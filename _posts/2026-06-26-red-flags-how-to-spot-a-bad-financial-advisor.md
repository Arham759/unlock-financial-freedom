---
title: "Red Flags: How to Spot a Bad Financial Advisor"
description: "Warning signs that indicate a financial advisor may not have your best interests in mind."
---

Most bad financial advice doesn't look obviously bad — these warning signs help catch it before damage is done.

## Reluctance to Disclose Fees Clearly
A trustworthy advisor explains exactly how they're compensated without hedging or vague answers when directly asked.

## Pushing Products Aggressively
Repeatedly steering conversations toward specific products, especially early in the relationship, can indicate commission-driven incentives.

## Avoiding the Fiduciary Question
Dodging or giving unclear answers about fiduciary status is itself a significant warning sign.

## Guaranteeing Investment Returns
No legitimate advisor can honestly guarantee specific investment returns — this claim alone should end consideration.

## The Bottom Line
Clear, direct answers to fee and fiduciary questions are the fastest way to separate trustworthy advisors from problematic ones.
